# Spreelo v143.103 — Shotstack content-sized HTML5 fix

## Problem
Shotstack began failing previously valid full-frame HTML5 product overlays with:

`html5 rendering failed for 1 of 1 clip(s)`

The failure was reproduced with an older Spreelo render that had previously completed successfully. A controlled Shotstack Playground test confirmed that the same product animation renders successfully when the HTML5 clip viewport matches the animated product content instead of using a full 1080x1920 HTML5 viewport.

## Fix
- Product HTML5 clip now uses the calculated product render dimensions (`safeWidth` x `safeHeight`).
- The HTML/CSS viewport is content-sized instead of 1080x1920.
- Product placement on the 1080x1920 video canvas is preserved with Shotstack `offset.x` / `offset.y`.
- Existing product size, top placement, GSAP 1.00 → 1.08 → 1.00 animation, drop shadow, text overlay, logo overlay, background video, MP4 output, FPS, quality and poster capture remain unchanged.
- No SQL migration.
- No new environment variables.
