# Spreelo v143.67

## One locked product pipeline across product formats

- Product post, product carousel, AI product ad and animated product Reel now resolve products through the same exact-product pipeline.
- A product is rebuilt from its exact product URL into one locked object containing brand, model/title, product type, variant/color, SKU/article identifier, price, exact product URL and verified main image.
- Legacy Store Map/catalog/category discovery may still help discover a product URL, but it is no longer the final source of product identity or media.
- Carousel sources that did not originate in the campaign web-research path are also reopened and locked before use. The carousel fails closed if five exact locked products cannot be produced.
- Product-content contract upgraded to `product-engine-v3-locked-object` and carries the richer identity fields through downstream generation.

## Admin product replacement and regeneration

- Admin can replace a product by pasting only the original product URL.
- Spreelo fetches the product name, brand, product type, variant/color, SKU, price and best verified main image automatically.
- New exact-product resolver endpoint: `/api/admin/post-approvals/resolve-product`.
- New single-product regeneration endpoint: `/api/admin/post-approvals/regenerate-product`.
- Product post, AI product ad and animated product Reel regeneration use the same production pipeline as automatic generation.
- Carousel regeneration resolves all five supplied product URLs through the same locked-product pipeline before rebuilding slides and copy.
- Rich product identity metadata is preserved when admin edits are saved.

## AI product ads: six layout families

AI product ads can now choose contextually between six visibly different layout directions:

1. Emotional hero
2. Clean premium
3. Benefits with icons
4. Problem → solution
5. Technical/specification
6. Seasonal campaign

The product/campaign context influences the family selection. The prompt no longer defaults to three feature bullets and explicitly avoids inventing benefits/specifications merely to fill a template.

## Professional admin review workspace

- Rebuilt review modal into a larger two-column review workspace.
- Quality overview for product identity, image quality, media count and post copy.
- Every review image is clickable and opens in a full-screen dark lightbox.
- Lightbox supports previous/next, keyboard arrows, Esc to close, image counter and original-product link when available.
- Carousel and single-product review cards show brand, product, type, variant, SKU/article ID and source-image dimensions.
- Product replacement uses an original URL + Fetch workflow instead of manual product-information fields.
- Sticky review action bar keeps Save, Regenerate and Release actions accessible.

## Animated product Reel identity presentation

- Reel text panels now use the same brand + model + product type/variant presentation model as other product formats.
- The old instruction that suppressed the product brand has been removed.

## Better high-resolution recovery for Drupal/Isover-style images

- Spreelo recognizes Drupal image-style derivatives such as `/files/styles/product_gallery/public/...jpg.webp` as derivatives of the same underlying product asset.
- Before accepting a small locked image, Spreelo tries the corresponding original asset URL from the same product image identity.
- This quality upgrade does not reopen page-wide image discovery and therefore does not reintroduce the old risk of selecting recommendation images from another product.
- If only the small verified source exists, the existing quality diagnostics remain available to admin review.

## Validation

Regression coverage includes product identity, fail-closed recovery, carousel reserve/finalization, admin regeneration, Pinterest carousel reliability, brand-aware labels, approval-page design and the new v143.67 unified product/review workflow.

No SQL changes are required for v143.67.
