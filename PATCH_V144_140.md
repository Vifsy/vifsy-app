# Spreelo v144.140 — Editorial Product Typography Polish

Built directly from v144.139.

## Fix
Product post typography has been tightened for both standard and transparent-source
`website_item` Product posts.

### What changed
- The Product post prompt now steers typography into a calmer **centered lower text block**.
- The main headline is now instructed to stay **shorter, more premium and clearly narrower than the full image width**.
- The prompt explicitly avoids **wide almost edge-to-edge headline treatments**.
- The prompt now discourages generic top-line formulas such as:
  - "Built for ..."
  - "Made for ..."
  - storefront CTA-style copy such as "Discover ..." / "Shop ..." / "Se produkten ..."
- When no locked headline is available from the post copy, the image prompt may create **one short unique editorial headline** instead of defaulting into repetitive generic phrasing.
- Caption-derived headline extraction now filters out weak/generic headline candidates so the visible copy lock does not keep promoting the same generic formula.

## Scope
- Applies to Product post (`website_item`) generation.
- Applies to both:
  - normal AI Content Studio Product posts
  - calendar-campaign Product posts
- Applies to both:
  - transparent-original composited products
  - non-transparent high-fidelity recreated products

No SQL is required.
