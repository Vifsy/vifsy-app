# Spreelo v144.111 — Rescue credit lifecycle + localized customer failure email

## What changed

### Failed posts no longer auto-refund
A terminal generation failure that is being held for Spreelo Admin Rescue no longer returns the customer's credit automatically.

- A previously reserved credit stays consumed/held for the failed occurrence.
- A legacy non-reserved occurrence is charged once when it enters rescue, if a customer balance exists and the failure is not `insufficient_credits`.
- Admin test jobs do not charge or refund customer credits.
- A recurring weekly plan may reserve a separate credit for its next future occurrence. The failed occurrence's credit and the next reservation are kept separate.

This prevents a manually rescued post from becoming free after its automatic attempt failed.

### Explicit admin cancellation and refund
Failed occurrences with a held rescue credit now show an admin credit card with:

- `X credits used for rescue`
- `Cancel and refund credit`

The action is explicit and idempotent. It:

1. refunds exactly the held rescue credit;
2. keeps any separate next-week reservation untouched;
3. records an audit event;
4. sends the customer a localized service email;
5. closes the failed work item only after the customer email succeeds.

If the refund succeeds but Resend fails, the refund is kept and Admin shows `Send customer email again`. Repeating the action never refunds twice.

A successfully rescued occurrence is marked `rescue_credit_resolved_with_post=true`, disables the refund action, and the SQL refund RPC also refuses to refund a rescue that already produced a replacement post.

### Localized customer email
When Admin gives up on a failed post and refunds it, the customer receives a clear non-technical email explaining:

- which kind of planned post could not be completed;
- an understandable reason category (website access, product verification, media generation, or technical dependency);
- how many credits were refunded;
- that the cancelled occurrence will not be tried again;
- that other planned content can continue unless a separate plan has been paused.

Technical failure codes are not exposed as the customer explanation.

### App language is authoritative for customer emails
`app_locale` / the user's selected Spreelo app language is now the first-choice locale for customer-facing service emails. Compatibility aliases such as `app_language` remain supported for older accounts.

Covered paths include:

- sign-in email (uses the selected login UI locale);
- post approval/review email;
- plan activation email;
- welcome email;
- analysis-completed email;
- annual calendar-updated email;
- rejected-post/customer confirmation email;
- trial-ending email;
- failed-post cancellation/refund email.

The Settings language selector now saves both `app_locale` and the legacy `app_language` field.

### No repeated AI translation cost per email
Curated static email copy remains preferred. For a supported app locale where some email strings do not yet exist in the file-backed pack, Spreelo creates the missing `emails` translation pack once globally and stores it in `ui_translation_packs`. Later sends reuse that pack and do not re-translate the same strings for every customer/email.
