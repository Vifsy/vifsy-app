# PATCH V144.142 — Editorial text centering, footer breathing room and logo fallback

## Problem
Two follow-up issues remained in editorial product posts:

1. the lower text stack could still feel slightly off-center because GPT-image-2 might center inside a locally imbalanced area instead of the full 4:5 canvas
2. the last text line could sit too close to the bottom edge with too little breathing room underneath
3. logo overlay depended on `logo_url` already being populated, so posts could skip the logo when a brand only had a saved `logo_storage_path`

## Fix
Updated the editorial product-post generation rules so both transparent and non-transparent product posts now:

- explicitly center the whole text stack on the full canvas
- keep equal-feeling left/right margins instead of drifting inside a shifted local text area
- keep the text inside a narrower centered column
- preserve a visible footer margin under the final text line

Also improved logo handling so brand logo overlays can now:

- use the existing `logo_url` when it is available
- fall back to generating a public URL from `logo_storage_path` in the `brand-assets` bucket when needed

## Files changed
- `app/api/cron/run-automations/route.js`

## Effect
- Editorial product text should now look more truly centered.
- The bottom line gets cleaner spacing underneath it.
- Brand logos are less likely to be skipped on generated posts when the brand has a stored logo asset.
- Affects both standard product posts and campaign product posts because they share the same generator and overlay flow.
