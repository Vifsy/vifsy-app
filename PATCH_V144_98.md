# Spreelo v144.98 — Campaign Overview Card In Unified AI Content Studio

- Added a new campaign overview card at the top of calendar-campaign planning inside the unified AI Content Studio.
- The overview explains what the campaign will focus on, shows the selected campaign period, the exact number of planned posts in the campaign, and clarifies that post dates can still be adjusted.
- The campaign image from the AI calendar is now shown directly in the planner overview, with a safe fallback to the generic calendar visual.
- Added a visual period rail with markers for the planned post dates so customers can immediately understand how the campaign is distributed across the chosen period.
- Added two guidance cards that separate what Spreelo selects automatically from what the customer can still change.
- Added responsive styling so the campaign overview remains clear from desktop down to mobile.
- No SQL changes.
