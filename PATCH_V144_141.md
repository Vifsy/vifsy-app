# PATCH V144.141 — Transparent product text safe-zone fix

## Problem
Transparent-source product posts could place typography too close to the reserved product area. Because Spreelo composites the original transparent product on top after GPT-image-2 generates the background+typography layer, letters could appear partly hidden underneath the product.

## Fix
Updated the editorial product-post prompt contract so transparent-product posts now:

- receive the locked product placement coordinates in the prompt
- treat that placement as a hard reserved no-text zone
- reserve a dedicated lower text zone below the product footprint
- keep a clearly visible gap between the product and the first line of text
- keep the headline/product-name/supporting-line in a centered, narrower text column
- avoid nearly full-width banner headlines

Also tightened the non-transparent product-post prompt so text must not sit behind, underneath or be cut by the product silhouette.

## Files changed
- `app/api/cron/run-automations/route.js`

## Effect
- Prevents the product from visually covering typography in transparent-source product posts.
- Keeps the composition smart and editorial while preserving the transparent-original workflow.
- Affects both normal AI Content Studio product posts and calendar/campaign product posts because they share the same generator.
